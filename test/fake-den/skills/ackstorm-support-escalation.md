# Support escalation

Delivered by the ACKstorm organization server.

## Severity

- **P1** — customer production down, no workaround. Page on-call immediately.
- **P2** — major degradation with a workaround. Same working day.
- **P3** — everything else. Next business day.

## Contacts

Escalate through the on-call rota, never by direct message to an individual.
Record every escalation in the incident tracker before contacting anyone.
